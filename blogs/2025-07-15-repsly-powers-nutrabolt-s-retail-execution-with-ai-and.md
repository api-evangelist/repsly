BOSTON, July 15th, 2025 – Repsly, Inc., a leader in retail technology, is transforming field merchandising and retail execution through its advanced AI and image recognition solutions. By automating data capture and simplifying complex manual processes, Repsly empowers brands to gain accurate, real-time insights that drive smarter business decisions. Nutrabolt, a global leader in sports performance and nutrition, exemplifies how Repsly’s cutting-edge technology elevates retail performance—equipping field teams with AI-powered tools to streamline workflows and optimize in-store availability.
