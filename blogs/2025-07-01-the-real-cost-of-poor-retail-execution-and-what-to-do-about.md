Retail execution—the way products are placed, presented, and promoted in-store—can make or break a CPG brand’s success. When done effectively it can help drive sales, enhance your brand’s image, and increase operational efficiency. Done poorly, it can cost you—in dollars, hours, relationships, and brand loyalty.
